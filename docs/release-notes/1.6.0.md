What's new in 1.6.0

- A new look: rounded corners on the tiles, the cards, the text boxes and the drop-down lists, and drop-down lists in the colors of the theme (no more white boxes).
- Alt accounts: the numbers no longer have up and down arrows (just type, or use the up and down keys), a tidier goal row and a clearer progress bar.
- The list of pages on the left can be made smaller (icons only) with the arrow at the top.
- Quick buttons at the bottom: choose in Settings which ones you want (connect all, pause Discord, screenshot, Health, ...).
- A "?" next to many settings explains them when you point at it.
- The bell at the bottom shows the last notifications, also after a restart.
- Drop a .rdp file on the panel (on the top bar, the list on the left or the bottom bars) to make a session from it.
- A short tour of the panel at the first start. Start it again with Ctrl+K and then "Start the tour".
- The window also remembers its place and size after you resize it.
- Alt accounts: the button "Export to Discord" sends all alts, with their numbers, goals, notes and hive pictures, and a total, to your Discord webhook (the one from Settings).

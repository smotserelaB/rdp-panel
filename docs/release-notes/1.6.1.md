What's new in 1.6.1

- The log can be filtered: choose a session, an event type (connected, disconnected, failed, ...) or look for a word, and choose the days (one day, 7 days, 30 days or all). The number of events is shown above the list.
- Export to CSV: the events on the screen (with your filters) go to a file.
- History of 14 days per session: on the Monitoring page every chart has a button "14 days", and Ctrl+K has "History (14 days): name". See connected time per day, drops per day and the daily average of CPU, RAM and FPS.

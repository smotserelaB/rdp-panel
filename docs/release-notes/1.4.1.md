What's new in 1.4.1

- Sound off now really turns the sound off: the switch "Sound on / off" at the bottom mutes everything the panel plays in the Windows volume mixer, so also the sound of your sessions (not only the alert sounds). You will see "RdpPanel" in the Windows volume mixer.
- Fixed: a session you added disappeared after a restart when a profile was active. A new session now joins the active profile, and renaming a session keeps it in its profiles.
- Remove a session from a list without removing the session itself:
  - Performance overview: the x on a card, or right-click.
  - Daily summary: right-click a session.
  - PC management: the button "Remove from this list", or right-click.
  - The right-click menu (and a button in PC management) shows the removed sessions again. In the session settings you can also choose where a session is shown.
- Alt accounts: the - and + buttons and the step box are gone; the cards, and all buttons in the panel, now have rounded corners.

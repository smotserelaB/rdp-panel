What's new in 1.3.0

- New tab "Alt accounts": add your alts with a name, a kind of alt, your stock of gumdrops, coconuts, jellybeans, cloud vials, bloomshakers, nightbells, nectar shower vials and smoking planters, and a picture of the hive. The totals of all alts are shown at the top.
- Overview: choose what to show (all, problems, offline, connected) and how to sort (own order, name, problems first, CPU, FPS).
- Pin a session to the top, or mute the notifications of one session (right-click on its header, or the menu).
- Compact view: small tiles with only the status, CPU and RAM.
- Double-click the header of a tile to show it large (and back).
- Activity window: see what the panel is doing with every session (connecting, waiting to reconnect, connected for how long, ...).
- The tray icon now has a color: green when all sessions on your screens are connected, yellow when some are, red when none are.
- Per session you can set your own CPU and RAM warning level (session settings).
- Frozen session warning: when a connected session shows no change in the picture for N minutes, you get a warning, and the panel can reconnect by itself. Off by default; an idle desktop looks frozen too.
- Remove sessions from the Monitoring page (the Remove button on its chart); Show all again brings them back.
